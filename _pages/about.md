I am a PhD candidate at the [Empathic Computing Lab](https://www.empathiccomputing.org/), [Auckland Bioengineering Institute](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/abi.html), University of Auckland, working on real-time multimodal emotion recognition. My doctoral research applies deep learning to the fusion of speech, physiological signals, and facial expression features for affective state estimation — with a focus on low-latency, clinically relevant deployment.

My work sits at the intersection of **machine learning**, **cybersecurity**, and **affective computing**. Before my PhD I worked as a Research Assistant at Massey University's Cybersecurity Lab, specialising in Generative Adversarial Networks for intrusion detection, malware analysis, and lightweight cryptography.

## Current Project

I am contributing to the **Tōku Hoa** project — a personalised AI agent for mental health support — through the Empathic Computing Lab. The system aims to provide scalable, empathic digital companionship by accurately perceiving emotional states in real time.

## Research Interests

- Multimodal emotion recognition from speech, EEG, and physiological signals
- Machine learning for cybersecurity — intrusion detection, malware classification, adversarial ML
- Lightweight cryptography for constrained and IoT devices
- Generative Adversarial Networks for data augmentation and threat modelling
