Tōku Hoa ("My Friend" in te reo Māori) is the primary project of the Empathic Computing Lab at the University of Auckland's Auckland Bioengineering Institute. The system aims to create a personalised digital companion capable of understanding a user's emotional state and responding with appropriate, supportive interaction.

## My PhD Contribution

My doctoral work centres on the multimodal fusion pipeline — combining speech prosody, physiological signals (EEG, GSR), and facial expression features into a unified representation for real-time emotion recognition. The core challenge is achieving low-latency inference without sacrificing accuracy across demographically diverse users.

## Why It Matters

Mental health support is severely under-resourced globally. An empathic agent that accurately perceives emotional states could provide scalable, accessible support in contexts where human intervention is unavailable or stigmatised.
