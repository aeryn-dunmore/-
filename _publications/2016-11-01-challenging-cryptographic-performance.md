Published in *Digital Forensics Magazine* Issue 29, this technical report examines cryptographic performance challenges in applied contexts. The work investigates how real-world deployment constraints — limited compute, memory pressure, and adversarial conditions — affect the practical security guarantees of standard cryptographic algorithms.

This paper is part of the early research strand that led to the Master's thesis and, ultimately, the Matrix Encryption Walks algorithm (2023).
