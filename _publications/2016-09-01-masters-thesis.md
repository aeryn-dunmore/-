This thesis investigates the application of graph-based computational systems to the design and improvement of cryptographic algorithms. Using the structural properties of graphs — connectivity, walk complexity, and matrix representations — as the basis for generating cryptographically secure keystreams and permutations.

Completed with First Class Honours (MCIS), Auckland University of Technology, 2016. This work directly seeded the Matrix Encryption Walks (MEW) algorithm published in *Cryptography* (MDPI) in 2023.
