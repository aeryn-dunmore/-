This paper applies graph-theoretic methods to the analysis of cryptographic algorithm performance. Graph representations of cipher state transitions are constructed and analysed for connectivity, walk diversity, and cycle structure, correlated with known cryptanalytic attack surfaces.

This conference paper is one of the founding contributions of the research thread that led to the Master's thesis and ultimately the Matrix Encryption Walks (MEW) algorithm published in *Cryptography* (MDPI) in 2023.
