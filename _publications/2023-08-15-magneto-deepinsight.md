The translation of traffic flow data into images for machine learning classification has been extensively explored. The method of translation, however, has a significant impact on classification success. This paper extends DeepInsight — originally developed for genomic image translation — and MAGNETO to the network intrusion detection domain.

The extended pipeline preserves semantic relationships between features through a manifold-learning layout step prior to pixel encoding, allowing convolutional neural networks to exploit inter-feature structure invisible to standard tabular classifiers.
