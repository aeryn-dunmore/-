This paper proposes a reconstruction-based anomaly detection approach using an LSTM-autoencoder trained exclusively on benign multivariate network time-series. At inference time, high reconstruction error signals potential DDoS activity. The approach is semi-supervised: it requires no labelled attack data during training, making it practical for deployment in novel threat environments.
